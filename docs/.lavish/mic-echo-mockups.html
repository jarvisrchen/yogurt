AUD-11: echo the mic to a second output device

Three ways to put "send my mic to BlackHole while recording" into yogurt. All three share the same backend. Pick one below, or mix.

What it does. While a meeting records, yogurt already owns a cpal mic input stream. The echo opens a cpal output stream on a chosen device (typically BlackHole, so Zoom/OBS/a DAW can read it) and copies mic frames to it with a small fixed buffer. Audio stays in-process and on-machine, and muting the mic mutes the echo too.

Backend (same for A, B, C)

cpal inputmic stream, already in yogurt-audio::mic
SPSC ringmic callback also pushes f32 frames here (only when echo is on)
cpal outputnew echo.rs: output stream on the chosen device, pops the ring, mono → N channels, zero-fills on underrun
routesGET /api/audio/output-devices; POST /:id/echo-device (hot-swap through the existing capture-thread AudioCommand channel, like audio-device)
settingsmic_echo_output_device ("" = off), mic_echo_buffer (512)
lifecycleopens with /start, tears down with stop; mute pauses it; failure to open shows the existing error banner, recording continues

Inspired by shadow-voice's single PortAudio duplex stream, but two cpal streams plus a ring is what the existing mic-capture ownership allows without touching the STT path. Sample-rate mismatch between mic and output goes through the existing resample.rs.
