Transcribe on the phone

Mocks and an evaluation for MOB-4. The phone gets the same choice the Mac has: Deepgram in the cloud, or a model that runs on the device. Screens are drawn with the webapp's own tokens and class strings at Pixel 10 Pro XL size (412 by 915 CSS px), light on the left and dark on the right. Meeting content is made up. Parts of a screen this change leaves alone, such as the Mac and label pickers on the record screen, are left out.

TodayEvery phone recording streams to Deepgram nova-3, from the Android app's recorder service or from the page in Chrome. The Settings screen has no transcription section.
The MacSettings has a Cloud card (Deepgram) and a Local card (whisper.cpp) with downloadable models. Use Local stays disabled until the chosen model is on disk.
ProposalThe Android app runs sherpa-onnx inside its recorder service, with Whisper and Parakeet models. Settings gets one Transcription row that opens a page with the Mac's Cloud and Local cards and model pills. Chrome keeps Cloud only.
