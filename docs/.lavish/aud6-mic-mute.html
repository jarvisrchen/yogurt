Mic mute toggle

A big, full-width button between the mic picker and the notes card, always visible. Pauses Channel::Mic only — system audio keeps recording. The M hotkey toggles it without touching the mouse.

Shipped 2026-09-01, revised from a small toolbar icon after review: mid-meeting mute is a core action, so it needed to be a bigger, always-findable target, plus a keyboard shortcut for reflex speed.
The comparison below the states grid shows what changed and why.

What changed — icon-only toolbar chip → big always-visible button

Considered first

28×28 icon, tucked into the mic-picker row, gone entirely while not recording. Matched the toolbar's density but was easy to miss for something you need mid-sentence.

Shipped

Full-width, always mounted (disabled when unusable), plus the M hotkey. Reads as a primary in-meeting control, not a settings toggle.
