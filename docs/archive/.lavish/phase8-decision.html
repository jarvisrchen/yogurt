⏱ The 3-second kill criterion

9 steps · expected duration ~15 min on M1 Air · LOCAL-04 + LOCAL-03 + offline
  1. Open http://localhost:7878/settings. Confirm the "Local · whisper.cpp" card replaces the old "Coming soon" stub.
  2. Click the Use Local radio. Matcha border appears.
  3. Click the small.en ↓ pill. The download dialog opens with live bytes / MB/s / ETA.
  4. After download, the pill flips to small.en ✓. shasum -a 256 ~/.yogurt/models/ggml-small.en.bin matches REGISTRY.
  5. Click + New meeting and talk for 30 s. Time the first transcript bar. Target: < 3.0 s.
  6. End the meeting. Confirm transcript persisted to library.
  7. Disable wifi, close browser, restart yogurt, start new meeting, talk — transcript must still flow.
  8. Re-enable wifi; in the browser, confirm cloud mode still works after the local round-trip (no settings corruption).
  9. Report: "approved" or "escalate to v2 — M1 Air bench failed at <Ns>".
