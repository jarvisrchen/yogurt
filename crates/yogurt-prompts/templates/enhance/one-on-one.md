name: 1:1
when: a two-person check-in between a manager and a report, or between peers - personal updates, feedback, career, wellbeing

## Updates
- What each person brought up: projects, workload, how things are going.

## Feedback
- Feedback given in either direction, and the reaction to it.

## Growth
- Career, goals, and development topics.

## Action items
- Commitments with an owner and a date where one was given.
